01 · The Project

A space built for equality needs technology that respects it.

The Statue of Equality complex at Hussain Sagar stands on a Parliament-replica pedestal: three floors, roughly 26,258 sq ft of circular gallery space, and a 125-ft bronze likeness of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar overlooking the city. Visitors will arrive in tens of thousands a year — many for the first time engaging seriously with his life and ideas. The interpretive software and content has to meet that scale without flattening the substance.

The Brief

Hardware (touchscreens, mounts, audio, network) is supplied by our partner. Our scope covers everything visitors will see and hear on those screens: the software shell, the content design, the photography, the films, and the language layers.

Two languages are non-negotiable: English and Telugu. Hindi is included by default. Marathi and Tamil can be added at content-only cost.

The Approach

Museum-grade interactive software is not a website on a touchscreen. It is a slow-paced, dignified, accessible interface that treats a visitor's attention as a gift, not a metric. Our design language draws from the Constitution itself — restraint, weight, and clarity.

Every kiosk is built on a single offline-first shell so a network outage never blanks a gallery. Content updates push through a small admin panel.

07 · Commercial Terms

The guardrails.

Validity

This proposal is valid for sixty (60) days from the date of issue. Pricing assumes a contract signed within this window; otherwise revised quotation will be issued.

Intellectual Property

Final delivered content (scripts, images, films, software builds) is transferred to the project authority on full payment. Source code is delivered under a perpetual, non-exclusive license. vkstudio retains the right to feature the work in its portfolio with credit.

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep proposal terms, financial arrangements, and unreleased content strictly confidential. A formal NDA can be executed on request.

Change Orders

Scope changes are accommodated through a written change-order process. Material changes (new galleries, additional languages, scope expansion) may impact timeline and price.

Jurisdiction

This engagement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts at Hyderabad, Telangana.

Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, network outages at venue, or government-imposed restrictions.
